# Sources and credits

## ToonTask reference data

Thank you to the editors and contributors of the [Toontown Rewritten Wiki](https://toontownrewritten.wiki/Main_Page). Their [ToonTask guide and linked tasklines](https://toontownrewritten.wiki/ToonTask#Taskline_guides) made it possible for The Mingler to offer detailed guidance in `/mytoons tasks` and `/mytoons comparetasks`, with task and progression context in `/mytoons profile`. The wiki is the community reference used for this ToonTask research. Toontown Rewritten's official release notes document when major game changes took effect.

I built up The Mingler's reference data over years of playing, recording observations, working with friends who contributed their findings, and collecting entries through bot commands. I organized selected wiki references into structured records for objectives, contacts, locations, rewards, and task steps. The Mingler uses its own logic to match those references to a Toon's tasks and compare shared objectives. A player's active tasks and progress come from their own Companion sync.

### Why the wiki matters

- **May 24, 2024 · Under New Management (4.0.0).** Toontown Rewritten rebalanced more than 200 late-game Donald's Dreamland tasks and revised tasks to reflect Cog Headquarters changes. [Official release notes](https://www.toontownrewritten.com/news/release-notes/357)
- **June 27, 2026 · The Re-Quested Update (4.4.0).** The update brought more than 50 new tasks, reworked Donald's Dreamland progression, and brought Lawbot and Bossbot disguise steps into that progression. It also added optional Gag XP tasks. [Official announcement](https://www.toontownrewritten.com/news/item/1108/toon-hq-the-re-quested-update-is-a-go)

A playthrough shows the tasks and choices that one Toon encounters. The community guides document many reward paths, objective variants, and contacts across updates. They make it practical to check the reference alongside firsthand observations. When The Mingler can't confirm a step, follow the current task in your Shticker Book.

Key guides include [Donald's Dreamland ToonTasks](https://toontownrewritten.wiki/Donald%27s_Dreamland_ToonTasks), [Lawbot Headquarters ToonTasks](https://toontownrewritten.wiki/Lawbot_Headquarters_ToonTasks), [Bossbot Headquarters ToonTasks](https://toontownrewritten.wiki/Bossbot_Headquarters_ToonTasks), and [Sellbot Task Force ToonTasks](https://toontownrewritten.wiki/Sellbot_Task_Force_ToonTasks).

## Official game releases

Use the [official Toontown Rewritten release notes archive](https://www.toontownrewritten.com/news/release-notes/all) to verify reported game changes by version and date. The Mingler's release-review baseline is **November 21, 2026**. Releases dated after that baseline are candidates for individual review. A release note can inform a proposed change after its relevance and behavior are checked. See the [Toontown Rewritten Terms of Service](https://www.toontownrewritten.com/play/terms-of-service) for the game's current rules.
